PHAN 1: MAN HINH BAN HANG POS & TRU KHO FEFO (PosCheckoutView)
----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------

[THAY NHAT HOI 1.1]:
"Thay thay o quay POS nay, khi khach mua mot mat hang ma trong kho co nhieu lo: lo con 3 ngay 
nua het han va lo con 30 ngay. He thong cua 2 em tu dong tru lo nao truoc? Code o dau va 
co khi nao bi tru lon xon khong?"

>> KICH BAN TRA LOI CUA DU & QUAN:
- "Da thua Thay, he thong cua tui em tuan thu tuyet doi nguyen tac FEFO (First Expired, 
  First Out) - Lo nao het han truoc se duoc tu dong xuat ban truoc.
- O Frontend (PosCheckoutView.jsx), nhan vien chi can chon san pham va so luong, khong 
  can phai nho ma lo.
- O Backend (FefoAllocationService.cs / InventoryRepository.cs), khi nhan request ban hang, 
  cau truy van SQL se loc cac lo co:
    WHERE product_id = @ProductId AND status = 'ACTIVE' AND quantity > 0
    ORDER BY expiry_date ASC, id ASC
  Nho menh de 'ORDER BY expiry_date ASC', lo co HSD gan nhat luon luon duoc lay ra truoc.
  Neu lo do khong du so luong, vong lap se tru tiep vao lo ke can cho den khi du so luong khach mua."
>> CHO DE SHOW CHO THAY COI:
- Frontend: PosCheckoutView.jsx (ham handleCheckout / dispatch)
- Backend: FefoAllocationService hoac Stored Procedure / Query co ORDER BY expiry_date ASC.

PHAN 2: MAN HINH THEO DOI FEFO & CANH BAO CAN DATE (FefoMonitorView)
----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------

[THAY NHAT HOI 2.1]:
"Tren man hinh FEFO Monitor, Thay thay co cac tag mau Xanh, Vang, Do. Cac em dua tren co so 
khoa hoc nao de gan mau? Co phai cu con 3 ngay la mau do khong?"

>> KICH BAN TRA LOI CUA DU & QUAN:
- "Da thua Thay, tui em khong hardcode so ngay co dinh ma tinh theo CONG THUC % TY LE 
  VONG DOI CON LAI (Remaining Shelf Life - RSL):
    RSL = (So ngay con lai den HSD / Tong han su dung ban dau) * 100%
- Trong do Tong han su dung = expiry_date - import_date.
- Quy uoc 3 nguong an toan:
  + Mau Xanh (Safe): RSL > 20% -> Hang an toan, ban binh thuong tren ke.
  + Mau Vang (Warning): 10% < RSL <= 20% -> Canh bao nhan vien quan sat, uu tien don ra truoc.
  + Mau Do (Critical): RSL <= 10% (hoac con <= 3 ngay) -> Canh bao khuyen mai xan hang 20% - 50% 
    hoac dao len ke vang truoc mat khach hang de kich cau.
  + Mau Tim (Expired): So ngay <= 0 -> Khoa ban, cho tieu huy."
>> CHO DE SHOW CHO THAY COI:
- File FefoMonitorView.jsx hoac spoilage-business-rules: Ham tinh `calculateRsl(importDate, expiryDate)` 
  va doan code render mau badge.

[THAY NHAT HOI 2.2]:
"Thay thay co cot 'Chi so nguy co lang phi' (DOS va DUE). DOS la gi, DUE la gi? No giup gi 
cho chu cua hang?"

>> KICH BAN TRA LOI CUA DU & QUAN:
- "Da thua Thay, day la diem sang sang tao trong de tai cua tui em ve chong lang phi:
  + DUE (Days Until Expiry): So ngay con lai truoc khi lo hang bi het han.
  + DOS (Days of Supply): So ngay can thiet de ban het luong ton kho hien tai, tinh bang 
    'Tong ton kho / Toc do ban trung binh 1 ngay (d)'.
  + NGUY CO LANG PHI: Neu DOS > DUE, nghia la voi toc do ban binh thuong cua quan, hang se 
    HET HAN TRUOC KHI KHACH MUA HET!
  + Vi du: Con 100 hop sua chua, 1 ngay chi ban duoc 5 hop -> can 20 ngay moi ban het (DOS = 20). 
    Nhung chi con 5 ngay nua la sua chua het han (DUE = 5). 
    He thong lap tuc bao dong Do: 'NGUY CO HU HONG CAO' va goi y chu cua hang giam gia 30% 
    ngay hom nay de nang toc do ban d len, tranh phai vut bo ton that 100% gia von."

PHAN 3: MAN HINH TIEP NHAN HANG TU KHO TONG (DcIntakeView)
----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------

[THAY NHAT HOI 3.1]:
"Cua hang ban le cua em nhan hang tu Kho tong DC, neu Thay co tinh go HSD be hon ngay nhap 
kho thi sao? Cac em validate o dau?"

>> KICH BAN TRA LOI CUA DU & QUAN:
- "Da thua Thay, he thong validate 2 lop (Dual-layer Validation):
  1. O Frontend (DcIntakeView.jsx): O nhap expiry_date co thuoc tinh min dat bang ngay hom nay 
     hoac import_date. Neu go ngay qua khu, form bao do va chan nut Luu.
  2. O Backend (BatchService.cs / FluentValidation / DTO): Kiem tra dieu kien 
     'expiry_date > import_date' va 'quantity > 0'.
  3. O Database: Bang batches co CHECK CONSTRAINT: 
     CHECK (expiry_date > import_date AND quantity >= 0).
  Vi vay du co hack qua mat Frontend cung khong the ghi du lieu sai vao Database."

[THAY NHAT HOI 3.2]:
"Tai sao khi nhan hang tu Kho tong lai bat buoc nhap 'cost_price' (gia von)? Gia von nay 
co tac dung gi trong phan mem chong lang phi?"

>> KICH BAN TRA LOI CUA DU & QUAN:
- "Da thua Thay, 'cost_price' la can cu duy nhat de:
  1. Tinh tong thiet hai tai chinh thuc te khi phai tieu huy lo hang bi hu/qua han:
     Thiet hai = So luong tieu huy * cost_price.
  2. Tinh chi so Ty le lang phi (Spoilage Rate %):
     Spoilage Rate = (Tong chi phi hang huy / Tong gia tri hang nhap) * 100%.
  Neu khong co gia von, he thong chi biet mat bao nhieu goi banh, chu khong the bao cao 
  cho giam doc biet cua hang dang bi chay tui bao nhieu trieu dong trong thang."

PHAN 5: MAN HINH DU BAO NHU CAU & TAI DAT HANG ROP (ReorderPredictorView)
----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------

[THAY NHAT HOI 5.1]:
"Cong thuc tinh diem dat hang ROP (Reorder Point) cua em la gi? Lead Time va Safety Stock 
lay tu dau?"

>> KICH BAN TRA LOI CUA DU & QUAN:
- "Da thua Thay, cong thuc ROP chuan trong quan tri chuoi cung ung la:
    ROP = (d * L) + SS
- Trong do:
  + d (Daily Demand): Suc mua trung binh 1 ngay cua san pham do trong 30 ngay qua.
  + L (Lead Time): Thoi gian tu luc gui don dat hang den khi Kho tong DC giao hang toi cua hang 
    (mac dinh mo hinh cua tui em la 1 - 2 ngay).
  + SS (Safety Stock): Ton kho an toan de tranh dut hang khi xe hang den tre.
- Khi Tong ton kho hien tai tren ke <= ROP, he thong se tu dong ban tin nhan: 'Can dat them 
  hang tu Kho tong'."

[THAY NHAT HOI 5.2]:
"Thay thay tren web co cac nut chon 'Troi nang', 'Troi mua', 'Ngay le'. May cai do de lam 
canh hay co tac dong that su vao ket qua du bao?"

>> KICH BAN TRA LOI CUA DU & QUAN:
- "Da thua Thay, do la he so dieu chinh ngoai canh (K Multipliers) tac dong truc tiep vao 
  toc do ban d:
  + Ngay thuong: K = 1.0.
  + Cuoi tuan / Ngay le: Suc mua tang, he so K = 1.25 den 1.60.
  + Mua nang nong: Cac nganh hang nuoc giai khat, kem duoc nhan them he so K = 1.40.
  + Mua mua bao: Nguoi dan it ra duong, hang tuoi song giam K = 0.75 nhung mi goi tang len K = 1.30.
- Nho nhan he so nay, don de xuat gui Kho tong khong bi tinh trang 'troi mua ma nhap thua 
  kem lam chay date', giup chong lang phi chu dong ngay tu khau nhap!"
>> CHO DE SHOW CHO THAY COI:
- Click chuyen doi giua "Ngay Nang" va "Ngay Mua" tren ReorderPredictorView de Thay thay 
  con so de xuat nhap tu dong thay doi lap tuc!
